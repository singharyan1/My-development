Method. This uses a commonly cited airport-industry rule of thumb of roughly 1,000 total jobs (direct, indirect and induced combined) per one million passengers handled per year, split here as about 40% direct, 35% indirect and 25% induced. Real outcomes vary widely by airport size, cargo volume, and how much industry grows around it.
